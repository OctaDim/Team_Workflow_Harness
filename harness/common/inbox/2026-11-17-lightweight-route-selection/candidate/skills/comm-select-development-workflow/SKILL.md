# Confirm one workflow route

Inputs: user request, authorized scope, available context, brief route descriptions,
prior menu and user choice. The comm-confirm-development-workflow rule determines
eligibility; research is included. Preserve an unambiguous choice for the same scope.

Before presenting routes read [menu](references/menu.md).
Before interpreting a reply read [choice](references/choice.md).
Prepare the compact menu from the request, existing context and brief descriptions;
do not load alternative workflows or their plugin/skill/rule instructions to compare.
Give a brief reason and recommendation without claiming a full procedural comparison.
Show the required options, ask for one route and wait; never choose automatically.
After explicit selection read and apply that route's necessary instructions;
unselected routes remain unloaded. Mandatory harness procedures still apply.
After either full Superpowers route is selected, before applying it read
[full process](references/full-superpowers.md); preserve the chosen executor.

Done: an unambiguous choice, or a precise question with dependent work stopped.
Other permissions and applicable instructions remain required.
