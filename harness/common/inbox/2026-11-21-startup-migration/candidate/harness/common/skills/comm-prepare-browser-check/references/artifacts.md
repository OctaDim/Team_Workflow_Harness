# Artifact identity

Identify intended revision/build and coherent JS/CSS/templates/assets. Verify
imports, serving roots and browser-loaded files; cwd/HEAD alone proves no
identity. Use suitable existing IDs/manifests, paths or fingerprints.

Comparisons account for all components affecting the contract; established
unchanged ones may be shared. Equal results alone prove neither source error
nor success. Record snapshot origin/version and intentional edits; difference
from latest does not invalidate an approved versioned reference.

Prototype: correct base paths/version URL; confirm critical assets load/apply,
not just HTTP success. Separate servers and a nested-path 404 are not universal.
Shared symptoms do not prove a launch fault.
